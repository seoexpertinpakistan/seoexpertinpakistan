Phase 04: On-Page Optimization

Every target page gets a full on-page SEO review and rewrite where needed. Title tags, headings, meta descriptions, internal linking, image optimization, and content structure are all addressed for every page in scope.

Content optimization goes past keyword placement. Search intent matching, semantic structure, and readability improvements are applied so each page genuinely answers what the searcher needs and gives them a reason to stay on your site.

Internal linking is mapped out so authority flows correctly across your site. Pages that need ranking power get more internal links directed at them, which reduces how many external backlinks they need to climb search results.
